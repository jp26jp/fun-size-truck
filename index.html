An unofficial keynote for the Slate Truck

Truck.

The first pickup you can pick up.

Shown actual size.

Can’t see it? Neither could the front row.

[reverent silence]

Look at it.

Super cute.

Like a Smart car with a truck bed that you can load other small things into.

A toaster. A houseplant. A shoebox. If it’s small, it’s welcome here, next to the other small thing.

[audible “aww”]

Where it all started

Hobbitville.

There used to be a place called Hobbitville by my house. (Garrett’s been.) They had all of these creepy houses because they were all fun size. But this car* could have easily fit in their fun-size garage.

*Truck.

Most people saw creepy little houses. A few of us saw a parking spot.

†Rated retroactively, from memory.

Houses: still creepy. Not a vehicle issue.

[knowing nods]

Introducing Fence Mode

Over.

It’s like the perfect vehicle for moving dirt from one side of the yard to another, and then you don’t need to worry about it getting around a fence, because you can just pick it up and put it on the other side.

★★★★★ “HA HA” (an early reviewer)

Look at that form. Look at that dirt.

Other trucks go around. This one goes over. No app. No update. No subscription. Just you, the truck and excellent posture.

We think you’re going to love it. We think you’re going to be able to lift it.

Fence Mode moves the truck, not you. Climb accordingly. Dirt not included. You already have dirt.

[gasps, then applause]

Introducing Favor Protection

All of the truck. None of the favors.

I think the biggest upside is you get to say “I drive a truck,” but never have to worry about anyone asking to use your truck to pick up something like a couch or a table, since they’re both bigger than your truck.

Favor Protection is always on. There’s no setting to find, because it’s built into the dimensions.

That’s not a limitation. That’s a boundary.

Favor Protection: always on

Capacity

Seats four.*

You could easily fit 4 table chairs, then rent a truck to pick up the table.

It’s not a shopping trip. It’s a series.

Dinner for four. Delivered in installments.

Family

Dual-zone family seating.

If you have 2 kids, one can ride in the seat next to you and the other can go in the bed.*

Zone 1: next to you. Zone 2: see fine print.

Introducing the Built-In Favorite Child Selector: two seats, one decision, every single morning.

Fine print (please actually read this one)

*Fine print, and we mean it: no kid rides in the bed. Not for one block, not in the driveway. If you have two kids, you have one more kid than this truck. Car-seat rules still apply to Kid #1; read them, they are longer than the bed. The seat map is a joke. The seatbelt is not.

[everyone reads the fine print]

Travel

The airport run. Retired.

If you have family come into town and there’s more than one, they’ll know they need to get an Uber. So no more of those annoying trips to the airport.

No circling arrivals. No “we’re at door 6.” No “the other door 6.” The truck says no, so you never have to.

Party of one? Technically, they’d fit. We’d keep that quiet.

FAQ

Questions. Small ones.

Is it really a truck?Show answerHide answer

It has a bed, so yes. You may say “I drive a truck” at parties, reunions and job interviews. If anyone follows up, “It’s a small one” ends the conversation.

Does it come in a bigger size?Show answerHide answer

It comes in actual size. Bigger than that? Yes. It’s called a truck.

Can I use it to help a friend move?Show answerHide answer

Only if your friend owns 4 chairs and nothing else.

What if there’s a fence in the way?Show answerHide answer

Pick it up and put it on the other side. Lift with your legs. For taller fences, ask a friend to help. Ask nicely. You’ve never had to help them move.

Can my second kid ride in the bed?Show answerHide answer

No. Not for a short trip, not at walking speed, not in the driveway. Every kid rides buckled into a real seat, every trip, in this truck or another one. The bed is for dirt and chairs.

My family is flying in. Do I have to pick them up?Show answerHide answer

If there’s more than one, they’ll know they need to get an Uber. No more of those annoying trips to the airport. If it’s just one, technically they’d fit. We’d keep that quiet.

Is this an official Slate Auto page?Show answerHide answer

No. It’s a parody made by friends in a group chat. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Slate Auto. The truck seems lovely. We just can’t stop measuring it.
